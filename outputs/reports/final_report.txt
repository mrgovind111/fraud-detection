3. METHODOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Preprocessing:
  - RobustScaler on Amount and Time (handles outliers)
  - V1-V28 already PCA-transformed (untouched)

Models:
  - Isolation Forest (tree-based, global anomalies)
  - Local Outlier Factor (density-based, local anomalies)

Training strategy:
  - IF: full training set
  - LOF: 20,000 subsample of normal transactions

7. RECOMMENDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Deploy LOF (n_neighbors=20) with threshold tuned to business
priority. Recommended operating points:

  - For high-risk segments (auto-block):
    Conservative threshold -> high precision, fewer false alarms

  - For standard flow (manual review queue):
    Balanced threshold (max F1) -> best precision/recall trade-off

  - For fraud-averse segments (flag & verify):
    Aggressive threshold -> catch most fraud, accept more FPs
